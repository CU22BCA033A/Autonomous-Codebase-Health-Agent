## 6. ERROR LOG SYSTEM

### 6.1 Template

| Date | Question (ref) | Topic | My answer | Correct answer | Why I got it wrong | Lesson | Reattempt date |

### 6.2 How to maintain it
1. **Log every error within 24 hours.** That includes MCQs you got wrong, MCQs you guessed right (mark them "G"), and every subjective question scoring below 70% of its marks.
2. **Classify the cause** using one of these codes:
   - **K** = knowledge gap
   - **C** = concept misunderstanding
   - **R** = reading or instruction error
   - **T** = time pressure
   - **S** = skill (drawing) weakness
   - **X** = careless
3. **Write the lesson as a rule** ("Always unfold in reverse order"), not as a feeling.
4. **Schedule reattempts** after 3 days and again after 10 days (spaced repetition). On a reattempt, solve the question cold before looking at your notes.
5. **Every Sunday:** count the codes. If R + X > 30% of your errors, slow down and use the "circle the constraints" habit. If S dominates, add 30 minutes of daily drawing for the weakest skill. If K dominates, add flashcards.
6. **Before each mock:** read the last 2 weeks of lessons (5 minutes).
7. **The final week:** read only the "Lesson" column.

## 9. FINAL 7-DAY STRATEGY (13–19 Dec) & FINAL 24-HOUR STRATEGY (19–20 Dec)

### Final 7 days: what to revise
- **Revise:** Vol 12 (the whole revision book), your error-log lessons, the Vol 5 Tier-A patterns, and the formulas in Vol 12 §6.
- **Practise:** one timed subjective set every 2 days; 20 minutes of light sketching on the other days.
- **Do one mock** on Sunday 13 Dec at 10:00 (the exam slot) to set your body clock.

  - new books or channels
  - learning new rendering media (e.g. watercolour, if you haven't practised it)

  - hard maths
  - comparing yourself with others on social media
