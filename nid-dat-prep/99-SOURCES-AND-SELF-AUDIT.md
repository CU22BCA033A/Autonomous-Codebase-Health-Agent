# SOURCES USED, RESEARCH LOG & QUALITY SELF-AUDIT

## A. Research method & limitations (read this)
- **Research date:** 7 October 2026.
- **Method:** web search across official NID document listings and education and news portals. Where possible, each claim that can change from year to year was cross-checked in two or more sources.
- **Limitation:** my research environment's network policy **blocked direct access to `admissions.nid.edu` and to most third-party sites**. Official facts were therefore taken from search-engine extracts of NID's own PDF documents (the URLs are listed below) and corroborated by education portals. **Please verify all dates, fees and rules on https://admissions.nid.edu before acting on them.**
- **Labels used in the package:** [OFFICIAL] · [REPORTED] · [TREND-BASED / INFERRED] · [PREDICTION] · [UNVERIFIED].
