## A. Number & Letter Series (1–15)

**LR-1.** 5, 11, 23, 47, 95, ?
(a) 190 (b) 191 (c) 189 (d) 193
**Ans: (b)**. Each term is ×2 + 1: 95 × 2 + 1 = 191. *Concept:* multiplicative series · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* logic warm-up · *Mistake:* answering 190 by forgetting the +1.

**LR-2.** 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?
(a) 30 (b) 36 (c) 49 (d) 35
**Ans: (b)**. Squares: 6² = 36. *Concept:* squares · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* pattern recognition · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-3.** 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, ?
(a) 15 (b) 17 (c) 19 (d) 16
**Ans: (b)**. Primes. *Concept:* primes · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* pattern · *Mistake:* answering 15 (15 = 3 × 5).

**LR-4.** 3, 6, 18, 72, ?
(a) 144 (b) 288 (c) 360 (d) 216
**Ans: (c)**. The multipliers are ×2, ×3, ×4, so the next is ×5: 72 × 5 = 360. *Concept:* increasing multiplier · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* pattern · *Mistake:* answering 288 (×4 again).

**LR-5.** 1, 8, 27, 64, ?
(a) 100 (b) 125 (c) 81 (d) 216
**Ans: (b)**. Cubes: 5³ = 125. *Concept:* cubes · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* volume intuition · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-6.** 10, 9, 7, 4, 0, ?

**Ans: (b)**. The differences are −1, −2, −3, −4, so the next is −5: 0 − 5 = −5. *Concept:* difference series · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* pattern · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-7.** 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?

**Ans: (c)**. Each term is n² + 1: 6² + 1 = 37. *Concept:* squares + 1 · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* pattern · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-8.** A, C, F, J, O, ?
(a) T (b) U (c) S (d) V
**Ans: (b)**. The gaps grow by one: +2, +3, +4, +5, then +6. O(15) + 6 = 21 = U. *Concept:* letter series · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* pattern · *Mistake:* miscounting the letter positions.

**LR-9.** Z, X, V, T, R, ?

**LR-10.** AZ, BY, CX, DW, ?

**LR-11.** 1, 2, 6, 24, 120, ?

**Ans: (b)**. Factorials: 6! = 720. *Concept:* factorial series · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* pattern · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-12.** 4, 6, 9, 13.5, ?

**Ans: (b)**. ×1.5 each step: 13.5 × 1.5 = 20.25. *Concept:* ratio series · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* proportional thinking · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-13.** 2, 4, 3, 9, 4, 16, 5, ?
(a) 20 (b) 25 (c) 36 (d) 6
**Ans: (b)**. The terms come in pairs (n, n²), so 5 is followed by 25. *Concept:* paired series · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* pattern · *Mistake:* answering 6.

**LR-14.** 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ?
(a) 18 (b) 20 (c) 21 (d) 26
**Ans: (c)**. Fibonacci: 8 + 13 = 21. (Fibonacci numbers relate to the golden ratio and to spirals in sunflowers.) *Concept:* Fibonacci · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* design and nature link · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-15.** Which term is wrong in the series: 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, 128?

## E. Syllogisms & Deduction (53–65)

**LR-53.** All potters are artists. All artists are observers. Conclusion: All potters are observers.

**LR-54.** Some designers are painters. Some painters are poets. Conclusion: Some designers are poets.

**LR-55.** All chairs are furniture. No furniture is edible. Conclusion: No chair is edible.

**LR-56.** No sculptor is lazy. Some students are sculptors. Conclusion: Some students are not lazy.
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow
**Ans: (a)**. The students who are sculptors are not lazy. *Concept:* syllogism · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* logic · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-57.** All logos are symbols. Conclusion I: Some symbols are logos. Conclusion II: All symbols are logos.
(a) Only I (b) Only II (c) Both (d) Neither
**Ans: (a)**. "All A are B" implies "some B are A", but not "all B are A". *Concept:* conversion · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* logic · *Mistake:* answering (c).

**LR-58.** Some fabrics are silk. All silk is natural. Conclusion: Some fabrics are natural.

**LR-59.** All cubes are prisms. Some prisms are hollow. Conclusion: Some cubes are hollow.

**LR-60.** If it rains, the street floods. The street is not flooded. Conclusion: It did not rain.
(a) Follows (b) Does not follow
**Ans: (a)**. Modus tollens: denying the consequent is valid. *Concept:* conditional logic · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* reasoning · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-61.** If it rains, the street floods. The street is flooded. Conclusion: It rained.

**LR-62.** Every NID student sketches daily. Riya does not sketch daily. Conclusion: Riya is not an NID student.

**LR-63.** No square is a circle. All circles are round. Conclusion: No square is round.

## G. Arrangements, Ranking, Directions & Puzzles (79–100)

**LR-79.** Five designers P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row facing north. Q is to the immediate right of P. R is at the left end. T is between R and P. Where is S?
(a) Right end (b) Second from left (c) Middle (d) Left end
**Ans: (a)**. Seats 1–5 from left: R = 1. T lies between R and P, and Q is immediately right of P, so the order begins R, T, P, Q. That leaves S at seat 5, the right end. *Concept:* linear arrangement · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* logic · *Mistake:* none.

**LR-80.** In a class of 40, Asha is 12th from the top. What is her rank from the bottom?
(a) 28 (b) 29 (c) 30 (d) 27
**Ans: (b)**. 40 − 12 + 1 = 29. *Concept:* ranking · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* logic · *Mistake:* answering 28.

**LR-81.** Six people A–F sit around a circular table facing the centre. A sits opposite D. B is to the immediate left of A. C sits opposite B. F is to the immediate right of D. Who sits opposite E?
(a) F (b) B (c) A (d) C
**Ans: (a)**. Number the seats 1–6 clockwise (seen from above). For someone facing the centre, their immediate left is the next seat clockwise and their immediate right is the next seat anticlockwise. A = 1, D = 4. B is immediately left of A, so B = 2. C is opposite B, so C = 5. F is immediately right of D, so F = 3. That leaves E = 6, and seat 3 (F) is opposite seat 6. *Concept:* circular arrangement · *Diff:* H · *Why NID:* rigorous spatial logic · *Mistake:* mixing up left and right for people facing the centre.

**LR-82.** A walks 10 m South, turns left and walks 6 m, turns left and walks 10 m. How far and in which direction is A from the start?
(a) 6 m East (b) 6 m West (c) 10 m North (d) 16 m East
**Ans: (a)**. Turning left from South faces East, so A walks 6 m east. Turning left from East faces North, and 10 m north returns A to the starting latitude. A ends 6 m east of the start. *Concept:* directions · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* spatial logic · *Mistake:* turning in the wrong direction.
