## A. Percentages, Ratio, Proportion (1–20)

**QA-1.** What is 25% of 480?
(a) 100 (b) 120 (c) 140 (d) 96
**Ans: (b)**. 480 ÷ 4 = 120. *Concept:* percentage · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* basics · *Mistake:* none.

**QA-2.** A ₹1,200 table lamp's price rises by 15%. What is the new price?
(a) ₹1,350 (b) ₹1,380 (c) ₹1,400 (d) ₹1,320
**Ans: (b)**. 1200 × 1.15 = 1380. *Concept:* % increase · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* everyday · *Mistake:* none.

**QA-3.** A ₹2,000 item is discounted 10%, and the discounted price is then raised by 10%. What is the final price?
(a) ₹2,000 (b) ₹1,980 (c) ₹2,020 (d) ₹1,800
**Ans: (b)**. 2000 × 0.9 × 1.1 = 1980, a net change of −1%. *Concept:* successive % · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* the classic trap · *Mistake:* assuming the two changes cancel out.

**QA-4.** Successive discounts of 20% and 10% equal a single discount of:
(a) 30% (b) 28% (c) 25% (d) 32%
**Ans: (b)**. 1 − 0.8 × 0.9 = 0.28. *Concept:* successive % · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* reasoning · *Mistake:* adding them to get 30%.

**QA-5.** By what percentage does a price rising from ₹80 to ₹100 increase?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 80% (d) 15%
**Ans: (b)**. 20 ÷ 80 = 25%. *Concept:* % change · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* data reading · *Mistake:* dividing by the new value (20 ÷ 100 = 20%).

**QA-6.** By what percentage does a price falling from ₹100 to ₹80 decrease?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 80% (d) 15%
**Ans: (a)**. 20 ÷ 100 = 20%. *Concept:* % change · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* contrast with QA-5 · *Mistake:* none.

**QA-7.** 12 is what percentage of 48?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 30% (d) 40%
**Ans: (b)**. *Concept:* % · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* basics · *Mistake:* none.

**QA-8.** Red and white paint are mixed in the ratio 2 : 3 to make 10 L of pink. How much red paint is used?

**Ans: (b)**. 10 × 2/5 = 4. *Concept:* ratio · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* colour mixing context · *Mistake:* none.

**QA-9.** ₹64 is divided in the ratio 3 : 5. What is the larger share?
(a) ₹24 (b) ₹40 (c) ₹38 (d) ₹32
**Ans: (b)**. 64 × 5/8 = 40. *Concept:* ratio · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* basics · *Mistake:* none.

**QA-10.** If a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5, find a : b : c.
(a) 8 : 12 : 15 (b) 2 : 3 : 5 (c) 4 : 6 : 5 (d) 8 : 12 : 10
**Ans: (a)**. Make b equal in both ratios: a : b = 8 : 12 and b : c = 12 : 15. *Concept:* compound ratio · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* proportion · *Mistake:* answering (b).

**QA-11.** On a 1 : 200 site plan, a wall measures 4.5 cm. What is its real length?

**Ans: (b)**. 4.5 × 200 = 900 cm = 9 m. *Concept:* scale · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* architecture drawings · *Mistake:* converting the units wrongly.

**QA-12.** On a map where 1 cm = 5 km, two towns are 12.4 cm apart. What is the real distance?

**Ans: (b)**. *Concept:* map scale · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* scale · *Mistake:* none.

**QA-13.** A 1 : 10 model of a chair is made. The real seat height is 45 cm. What is the model seat height?
(a) 45 mm (b) 4.5 mm (c) 450 mm (d) 4.5 m
**Ans: (a)**. 45 cm ÷ 10 = 4.5 cm = 45 mm. *Concept:* model scale · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* Studio Test models · *Mistake:* mixing up cm and mm.

**QA-14.** A table top measures 50 cm² on a 1 : 20 model. What is the real area?
(a) 1,000 cm² (b) 2 m² (c) 0.2 m² (d) 20 m²
**Ans: (b)**. Areas scale by 20² = 400: 50 × 400 = 20,000 cm² = 2 m². *Concept:* area scaling · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* scale reasoning · *Mistake:* multiplying by 20 only.

**QA-15.** If every length of a box is doubled, its volume becomes:
(a) 2× (b) 4× (c) 6× (d) 8×
**Ans: (d)**. 2³ = 8. *Concept:* volume scaling · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* packaging · *Mistake:* answering 2×.

**QA-16.** Five artisans make a set of baskets in 12 days. How long would 6 artisans take at the same rate?
(a) 10 days (b) 14 days (c) 11 days (d) 9 days
**Ans: (a)**. Total work = 5 × 12 = 60 artisan-days; 60 ÷ 6 = 10. *Concept:* inverse proportion · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* planning · *Mistake:* using direct proportion.

**QA-17.** 3 pencils cost ₹12. How much do 15 pencils cost?
(a) ₹45 (b) ₹60 (c) ₹50 (d) ₹36

**QA-18.** A stall buys a mug for ₹400 and sells it for ₹500. What is the profit percentage?
(a) 20% (b) 25% (c) 10% (d) 100%
**Ans: (b)**. Profit % is calculated on the cost price: 100 ÷ 400 = 25%. *Concept:* profit · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* basics · *Mistake:* calculating on the selling price (20%).

**QA-19.** A rectangle's length increases by 20% and its width by 10%. By what percentage does its area increase?
(a) 30% (b) 32% (c) 28% (d) 22%
**Ans: (b)**. 1.2 × 1.1 = 1.32. *Concept:* combined % · *Diff:* M · *Why NID:* resizing artwork · *Mistake:* adding to 30%.

**QA-20.** A poster's width : height is 3 : 4. If the width is 45 cm, what is the height?
(a) 55 cm (b) 60 cm (c) 50 cm (d) 64 cm
**Ans: (b)**. 45 × 4/3 = 60. *Concept:* aspect ratio · *Diff:* E · *Why NID:* layout · *Mistake:* none.

**QA-29.** A bar chart shows the visitors to a craft fair: Mon 200, Tue 300, Wed 250, Thu 450, Fri 300. On which day did visitors increase the most compared with the previous day?

**QA-48.** A 200 m train moving at 72 km/h passes a pole. How long does it take?

**QA-50.** A cyclist goes 60 km at 30 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. What is the average speed for the whole trip?

**QA-51.** Walking at 5 km/h, how long does it take to cover 2.5 km?

**QA-52.** A can paint a mural in 6 days and B can paint it in 12 days. How long do they take working together?

**QA-53.** A tap fills a tank in 4 h and a drain empties it in 6 h. With both open, how long does the tank take to fill?

**QA-54.** What is the angle between the clock hands at 4:00?

**QA-57.** A bus leaves every 15 minutes from 6:00 am. How many buses leave between 6:00 am and 8:00 am, inclusive of both times?
